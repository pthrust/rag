# Xilar Nebula

Xilar Nebula is a nebula on the outskirts of the galaxy, hiding the only inhabited 
planet — **Xilar Prime**. For a long time it was considered uninhabitable due to 
dense ionized clouds that blocked scanners and made navigation nearly impossible. 
That is why fugitives from Imperium Dominus took refuge here.

## Geography
Xilar Prime is a rocky planet with low gravity and a turquoise sky. Its surface is 
covered with crystalline formations that reflect the light of the nebula and create 
the effect of "singing rocks" — a low-frequency hum audible for several kilometres. 
The atmosphere is breathable but saturated with ions, causing Mechanoid devices to 
operate unstable.

## History
- **Stellar Concord Era** — used as a secret observation station of the Luminari 
  Order for the far frontiers.
- **Edict of Annihilation** — several surviving Luminari took refuge here, including 
  the young Theron Aldric. He spent two years on Xilar Prime studying ancient 
  holocrons before moving to Dustfall.
- **Imperium Era** — the planet was listed in archives as "uninhabitable", allowing 
  the Freedom Coalition to use it as a transit point for scouts.
- **After the Fall of Imperium** — Xilar Prime became a centre for Aetherium research. 
  Elder Zorrin founded the first school here for gifted children not affiliated with 
  the Luminari Order.

## Flora and Fauna
- **Crystal Vines** — plants that absorb the ionized energy of the nebula and emit 
  a soft light. Used to create low-rank Photon Blade crystals.
- **Nebular Rays** — large flying creatures soaring in the upper atmosphere. Their 
  scales are used in Aegis Strike Fighter armour.
- **Xilar Moths** — tiny insects that migrate along the crystal formations. Considered 
  a symbol of hope by the local inhabitants.

## Significance to the Story
Xilar Nebula is mentioned as "the last refuge of the Aetherium" — a place where the 
Aetherium manifests especially strongly. Legends say that here will be born the one 
who will unite the scattered remnants of the Luminari after the fall of Imperium. 
After the events of the Moonfall Engagement, part of the Lyra Voidwalker Academy 
relocated here.
