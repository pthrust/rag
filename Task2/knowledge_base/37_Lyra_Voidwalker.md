# Lyra Voidwalker

Lyra Voidwalker is a young pilot of the Freedom Coalition, born on Verdant Moon. 
She is the daughter of a Furling guide and a volunteer pilot who died during the 
Glacier Siege. From childhood, she dreamed of becoming an Aegis Strike Fighter pilot 
and joining the fight against Imperium Dominus.

## Training
After the fall of Imperium, Lyra was accepted into the newly revived Luminari Order, 
where her personal mentor was Orin Lightseeker himself. He taught her the basics of 
Aetherium control and the art of piloting. Although Lyra did not possess as strong a 
connection to the Aetherium as her teacher, she compensated with tactical thinking 
and composure in battle.

## Key Events
- **Moonfall Engagement** — at the age of 16, she participated as a wingman in an 
  Aegis Strike Fighter squadron, covering the Freedom Coalition flagship.
- **Patrols on Verdant Moon** — after the victory over Imperium, she guarded trade 
  routes from pirates and remnants of the Shadow Cabal alongside a Furling squad.
- **Diplomatic Mission to Azure Sphere** — she accompanied Senator Elara Voss in 
  negotiations with the amphibious inhabitants, where she unexpectedly displayed a 
  talent for diplomacy.

## Personal Traits
Lyra is known for her loyalty to the Freedom Coalition, her daring humour, and her 
ability to find unconventional solutions. Her callsign — "Voidwalker" — was earned 
after she single-handedly piloted a damaged Aegis Strike Fighter through the wreckage 
of the destroyed Void Engine to save a wounded comrade.

## Legacy
She is considered one of the first pilots of the new generation of Luminari. A memorial 
was erected in her honour on City-Core, and the Freedom Coalition Pilot Academy bears 
her name — **Lyra Voidwalker Flight Academy**.
